# Sprint 8.6 – 247SP Production Readiness

## Objective

Transform 24/7 Sales Partner from a functional prototype into a production-ready digital front-office platform capable of supporting the first paying customer.

No new Ultimate Back Office modules will be developed during Sprint 8.6. Development effort is focused exclusively on completing the operational workflows required to sell, provision, support, and operate 24/7 Sales Partner as the launch product.

24/7 Sales Partner is no longer scoped as only a website builder. The production-ready product includes a done-for-you website, domain, professional email, local business phone number, AI receptionist, business texting, AI website chat, LeadHub CRM, and a unified conversation inbox.

LeadHub is the central system of record for every 247SP communication channel.

---

# Goals

Complete every workflow required for a customer to:

* Purchase 24/7 Sales Partner
* Complete onboarding
* Receive a working website
* Receive a domain, professional email, and local business phone number
* Capture leads from website forms, AI website chat, calls, AI receptionist interactions, texts, and supported email lead activity
* Manage every lead and conversation in LeadHub
* Use one unified conversation inbox
* Maintain billing
* Operate without manual database changes

---

# Milestone 1 — Lead Capture And Conversation Pipeline

## Objective

Every lead or conversation generated by a 247SP channel must automatically enter or update LeadHub.

### Deliverables

* Website contact forms submit successfully.
* AI website chat conversations create or update LeadHub records.
* Inbound call and AI receptionist summaries create or update LeadHub records when phone integration is active.
* Business texts create or update LeadHub records when texting integration is active.
* Supported email lead activity creates or updates LeadHub records when email integration is active.
* Contact form validation.
* Spam protection.
* Lead/contact creation.
* Lead assignment.
* Lead activity history.
* Conversation channel attribution.
* Email notification to customer.
* Internal notification.

### Validation

* Submit form.
* Lead appears in LeadHub.
* Conversation activity appears in the unified inbox.
* Customer receives notification.
* Activity logged.

### Milestone 1 Deferred Item

Email notification to the customer and internal notification remain deferred until shared notification infrastructure is implemented safely. Milestone 1 lead capture should create LeadHub contact, note, task, conversation, and activity records without sending real email.

# Exit Criteria

Sprint 8.6 is complete when a brand-new customer can purchase 24/7 Sales Partner, complete onboarding, receive a functioning front-office setup, generate leads, manage conversations in LeadHub, follow up through the unified inbox, and maintain their subscription without requiring manual intervention from the development team.
